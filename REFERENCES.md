## Reservoir computing / echo state networks
2. Jaeger, H. (2001). *The "echo state" approach to analysing and training
   recurrent neural networks.* GMD Report 148. `[jaeger2001]`
3. Jaeger, H. (2002). *Short term memory in echo state networks.* GMD Report 152.
   — basis of the Memory-Capacity metric. `[jaeger2002]`
4. Maass, W., Natschläger, T., & Markram, H. (2002). *Real-time computing without
   stable states: A new framework for neural computation based on perturbations.*
   Neural Computation, 14(11), 2531–2560. `[maass2002]`
5. Lukoševičius, M., & Jaeger, H. (2009). *Reservoir computing approaches to
   recurrent neural network training.* Computer Science Review, 3(3), 127–149.
   `[lukosevicius2009]`
6. Bertschinger, N., & Natschläger, T. (2004). *Real-time computation at the edge
   of chaos in recurrent neural networks.* Neural Computation, 16(7), 1413–1436.
   — edge-of-chaos / spectral-radius criterion. `[bertschinger2004]`
